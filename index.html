Diet how they chow, converted to grams per day

Searching USDA FoodData Central needs an API key. Without one the app uses USDA’s public demo key, which allows about 10 requests an hour for everyone on your network. Built-in ingredients never need a key.

get a free key

The amount field takes simple math, handy for batches: 400*2/10 is 400 g in a 10-cup batch fed 2 cups a day. Pick a unit and how often it is fed; everything is converted to grams per day. Values per 100 g come from USDA FoodData Central, manufacturers, or estimates (marked ~). Changes are saved in this browser, and the address bar always links to the current diet.
